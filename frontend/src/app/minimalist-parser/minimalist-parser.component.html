Project

This is a parser developed by Meaghan Fowlie at Utrecht University. It produces syntactic parses of English sentences in accordance with the framework of Chomskyan Minimalism.

The model in this demo is trained on about 80,000 sentences mostly from the Wall Street Journal. The trees are derived from John Torr's MGBank (opens in a new tab)
